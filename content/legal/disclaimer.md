## Not a substitute for professional advice

Website content does not replace an examination, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified dentist or doctor. Every patient is different. Please consult a dentist before making any decision about your oral health, and always follow the specific advice given to you at your appointment.

## No guarantee of results

Descriptions of treatments, typical numbers of sittings and timelines are general guides. Actual treatment, duration and outcomes depend on your individual condition and are discussed with you at consultation. No specific result is guaranteed.

## Emergencies

If you have a medical emergency — such as difficulty breathing or swallowing, rapidly spreading facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding or a serious injury — go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.

## Medicines

Any mention of pain relief or other medicines is general. Take medicines only as prescribed or as directed on the pack, and tell your dentist about any allergies, medical conditions or medicines you already take.
