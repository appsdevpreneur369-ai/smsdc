This Privacy Policy explains how **{{officialName}}** ("the clinic", "we", "us"), {{address}}, collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you use this website, contact us, or book an appointment. We aim to follow the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** ("DPDP Act") and the rules made under it.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The clinic is the *Data Fiduciary* for personal data collected through this website and at the clinic. For any privacy question or request, contact us at **{{email}}** or **{{phone}}**.

## 2. What we collect

- **Contact details** you give us — name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address.
- **Appointment details** — the problem you describe, preferred doctor, preferred date and time.
- **Health information** — dental and medical history, X-rays, clinical photographs and treatment records, collected at the clinic when you receive care.
- **Technical information** — basic data such as browser type and pages visited, if analytics are enabled (see our Cookie Notice).

We do not ask for more data than we need for the purpose described.

## 3. Why we use it

- To respond to your enquiries and manage appointments.
- To provide dental care, keep accurate clinical records and meet our legal and professional obligations.
- To send you appointment confirmations, reminders, follow-ups and aftercare messages.
- With your consent, to ask for feedback or a review.

We process your data on the basis of your **consent**, or for other purposes permitted by the DPDP Act, such as medical treatment and compliance with law.

## 4. WhatsApp, SMS and email messages

When you book or share your number with us, you consent to receive messages about your appointments and care by WhatsApp, SMS, email or phone. You can opt out of non-essential messages at any time by replying **STOP** or telling our front desk. Some service messages, such as appointment confirmations, may still be sent while you have an active booking.

When you contact us via WhatsApp, your messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

## 5. Clinical photographs

We may take photographs of your teeth for diagnosis, treatment planning and your records. We will **never publish** any photograph that could identify you — including on this website or social media — without your separate, written consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

## 6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only when necessary:

- With the doctors and staff involved in your care.
- With service providers who process data on our behalf, such as our appointment and messaging software (ClinicFlow247) and its messaging partners, dental laboratories and diagnostic centres, under appropriate safeguards.
- When required by law, a court order or a public authority.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 7. How long we keep it

Clinical records are kept for as long as required by applicable medical record-keeping rules and professional guidelines. Other data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, and then deleted or anonymised.

## 8. How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards — access controls, secure software and staff confidentiality — to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. In the event of a personal data breach, we will act as required by the DPDP Act.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

- Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
- Ask us to correct, complete or update your data.
- Ask us to erase data that is no longer needed, subject to our legal obligation to keep medical records.
- Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing already done).
- Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
- Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

## 10. Children

We collect personal data of children only with the consent of a parent or lawful guardian, and only to provide care.

## 11. Grievances

Please write to **{{email}}** with the subject "Privacy". We will acknowledge your request and respond within the time required by law.

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date shows when it was last changed.
