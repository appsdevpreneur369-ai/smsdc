This notice explains how the **{{officialName}}** website uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, which websites use to remember settings.

## What this website uses

- **No first-party cookies:** this website does not set its own cookies. Your language choice (English or Telugu) is part of the page address, not stored on your device.
- **Embedded services:** the map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.
- **Analytics:** this website does **not** currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent first.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking third-party cookies may stop the embedded map from loading; you can still open our location directly in Google Maps.
