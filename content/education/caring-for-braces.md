Braces work best when the teeth and gums around them stay healthy. A few habits make a big difference.

## Keeping clean

- **Brush after every meal** if you can, and at least twice a day. Angle the brush above and below the brackets.
- **Use an interdental brush** to clean between the wires and brackets.
- **Floss daily** with a floss threader or orthodontic floss.
- A fluoride mouthwash may give extra protection — ask your orthodontist.

## Foods to avoid

- **Hard foods:** nuts, hard sweets, ice, corn on the cob, biting straight into hard fruit (cut it into pieces instead).
- **Sticky foods:** toffees, chewing gum, chikki.
- **Sugary drinks** between meals, which raise the risk of marks and decay around brackets.

## Common niggles

- **Soreness** for a few days after fitting or adjustment is normal. Soft food and the pain relief your orthodontist recommends can help.
- **A wire poking your cheek:** cover it with orthodontic wax and call us.
- **A loose bracket:** keep it if it comes off and let us know before your next visit.

## Keep your appointments

Regular reviews — usually every 4–6 weeks — keep treatment on track. When your braces come off, wearing your retainer as advised helps keep your new smile.
