Some dental problems can wait a few days for an appointment. Others need attention the same day.

## Call us the same day if you have

- **Severe or throbbing toothache,** especially if it keeps you awake
- **Swelling of the gum or face**
- **A knocked-out adult tooth** — the first hour matters most
- **A broken or cracked tooth** with pain or sharp edges
- **Bleeding** after an extraction that doesn't stop with firm pressure
- **A lost filling or crown** that is causing pain

## Go to a hospital emergency department if you have

- Swelling spreading towards the eye, down the neck or under the tongue
- Difficulty breathing, swallowing or opening your mouth
- A high fever with facial swelling
- A significant injury to the face, jaw or head

## Don't ignore these, but book a regular appointment

- Sensitivity to hot or cold that lasts
- Bleeding gums
- A mouth ulcer that hasn't healed in two weeks
- A lump, or a red or white patch in the mouth

Early treatment is usually simpler and more comfortable. If you're unsure, call us and ask.
