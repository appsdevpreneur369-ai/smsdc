Good daily care keeps dentures comfortable, fresh and long-lasting — and protects your gums.

## Daily care

- **Take them out and rinse** after eating to remove food.
- **Brush them every day** with a soft brush and mild soap or denture cleaner. Regular toothpaste can be too abrasive.
- **Clean your gums, tongue and any natural teeth** with a soft brush.
- **Remove them at night** unless your dentist advises otherwise, and keep them in water or denture solution so they don't dry out.
- **Handle them over a folded towel or a basin of water** so they don't break if dropped.
- **Never use hot water**, which can warp them.

## Getting used to new dentures

- It's normal for new dentures to feel bulky at first, and for eating and speaking to take practice.
- Start with soft foods cut into small pieces, and chew slowly using both sides.
- Reading aloud can help your speech adjust.
- If an area becomes sore, come back for an adjustment — don't try to adjust dentures yourself.

## Regular check-ups

Your gums and jawbone slowly change shape over time, so dentures can become loose. See us once a year, or sooner if your dentures feel loose, crack, or cause sores that don't heal within two weeks.
