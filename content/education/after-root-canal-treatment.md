Root canal treatment removes infection from inside the tooth so you can keep it. Here's how to look after it afterwards.

## What to expect

- The tooth and gum may feel **tender for a few days**, especially when biting. This usually eases steadily.
- If your treatment is being done over more than one sitting, a **temporary filling** will be placed between visits.

## Looking after the tooth

- **Wait until the numbness wears off** before eating.
- **Avoid chewing hard food on that side** until the final filling or crown is placed. A treated tooth can be more brittle.
- **Keep brushing and flossing** normally, gently around the tooth.
- Take any medicines as prescribed and complete the full course of antibiotics if you were given them.

## Why a crown is often advised

Back teeth take a lot of chewing force. After root canal treatment they can become more likely to crack. A crown fits over the tooth like a protective cap and may help it last much longer. Your dentist will advise whether you need one.

## Call us if

- Pain or swelling gets worse instead of better after two or three days
- The temporary filling comes out
- Your bite feels uneven
- You notice a rash, itching or other reaction to a medicine
